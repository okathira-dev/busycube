# Busycube: Web API Explorer

[日本語](./README.md) · English

> Your everyday browser becomes the puzzle.

Busycube is a puzzle game where the browser itself is the key. Explore tabs, URLs, files, and your device's responses to find a way to open the boxes.

[Play Busycube](https://busycube.okathira.com/?locale=en) · [日本語で遊ぶ](https://busycube.okathira.com/?locale=ja)

## How to play

Choose a stage and look for clues around its boxes. Try the everyday features of your browser and device—you might find a way to open a box outside the game itself.

Stages are grouped by what you need to play:

- **Play now**: Stages that don't request browser permissions. These have been playtested in Chrome on Windows.
- **Permission required**: Stages that need access to features such as your camera or microphone.
- **Limited browser support**: Stages available only in certain browsers or environments.

Stages under "Permission required" and "Limited browser support" are in preview. Their content and presentation may change.

Available stages depend on your browser, operating system, device, installation status, and connected hardware.

## Permissions and progress

Some stages request access to your camera, microphone, location, notifications, or external devices. If you decline, you can still play other stages.

Progress is normally saved in your browser. You can optionally back it up to Google Drive's app-specific storage. See the [Privacy Policy](https://busycube.okathira.com/privacy/?locale=en) and [Terms of Use](https://busycube.okathira.com/terms/?locale=en) for details.
